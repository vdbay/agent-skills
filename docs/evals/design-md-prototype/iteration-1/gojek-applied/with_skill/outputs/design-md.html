Gojek / design system / v1.0 concept

One green.
Two registers.

A unified system for the consumer app and the corporate/technology platform: warm at the surface, precise underneath. One token core, two documented expression deltas, and no second brand.

Principles with consequences

A principle that cannot change a decision is decoration. Each of these changes what the prototype does, not only how it looks.

01

One core, two registers

One primary, one type family, one 4px grid. Registers vary density, accent proportion, type scale, tone, and motion intensity — never the core identity.

02

Brand green is a field

#00AA13 is for large graphics, fills, and display type. #00880D carries 14–16px labels and links. The brighter green measures 3.11:1 on white; the product green measures 4.64:1.

03

The route line connects

Draw the 2px route only between real moments. It is a narrative device, not a background texture. If it does not connect two stops, it does not belong.

04

Motion explains state

120ms for feedback, 200ms for a state change, 320ms for a route reveal. Transform and opacity only. Reduced motion becomes an immediate swap.

05

Evidence over vibes

Every colour value is traceable to a first-party source or marked as an assumption. The prototype labels sample scenarios and illustrative system views.

06

Density is a register decision

Consumer sections breathe at 96–128px; corporate sections compress to 64–96px. The spacing unit stays 4px; only the rhythm changes.

Two registers, one brand

gojek.com and gojek.io are treated as one company with two audiences. The register matrix is normative: it explains what changes and what must not.

Elevation and depth

A perpendicular light source, three shadow levels, and a dark theme that uses value steps and borders instead of black drops.

Low

Resting cards, sticky bars, the day panel.

0 2px 20px rgba(0,0,0,.08)

Mid

Hovered cards, popovers, active detail.

0 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,.10)

High

Dialogs, mobile sheet, toast. One at a time.

0 16px 64px rgba(0,0,0,.12)

Dark theme is not an inversion

Base #111313, raised #1C1D1D, border #6B6D6D. The value step and the 1px border do the work; shadows drop to 42–62% opacity. The green accent becomes #00AA13 with #08210D label text.

Shapes and radii

Pill actions, soft containers, circular route nodes. A sharp corner is reserved for data, not used as a second personality.

Action controls are always full-radius pills with a 2px border on secondary actions. Container radii run 16–24px; a card's radius should not exceed its padding. Inputs use 6–8px. Tables and code blocks use 0px. Icons are 24px on a 4px grid, 2px stroke, rounded caps. Route nodes are circles; a selected node may grow, but the line stays 2px.

Toast

The toast uses role="status", appears for four seconds, and can be dismissed by waiting. It is a live region, not a decoration.

Focus ring

Tab to any button, tab, or link on this page. The ring is 3px: ink on light surfaces, light on dark surfaces, and ink with a white halo on brand-green panels. The halo alone is not the focus signal; the 3px ring carries the contrast requirement.

# Gojek — One Green, Two Registers

## Overview

Gojek is one company with two public faces: a consumer super-app that helps people move, eat, send, shop, and pay, and a corporate/technology platform that serves businesses, partners, and the systems behind those moments. This system treats both as **one brand with two registers**, not two brands.

**Design thesis:** For people moving through an Indonesian day and for the teams building the systems behind it, Gojek should feel like one continuous green route — warm and human at the surface, precise and legible underneath.

The organising visual idea is the **route line**. On the consumer surface it is a day: a pickup, a meal, a package, a payment. On the corporate surface it is a system: a request, a match, a handoff, a confirmation. The same 2px line and circular nodes carry both stories.
