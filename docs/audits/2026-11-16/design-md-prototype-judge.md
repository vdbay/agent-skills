1. **`design-md.html:2940-3063` — make the source excerpt unambiguous.** It is a partial frontmatter with an in-code omission comment (`:3051`) and a closing `---` that does not exist at that point in the real file. Move the "selected excerpt" note outside the `<pre>` or show the full frontmatter plus one section; a reader should not be able to mistake the excerpt for a complete DESIGN.md. 3. **`design-md.html:2871-2874` — make the "Don't" visual show the actual failure.** The claim is that brand green at 12px on white is 3.11:1, but the sample text is rendered in `--color-error-text` and only the swatch is green.
